## Core market signal & innovation

Synthesis of Agility PR's saved trend topics reveals three structural shifts in how enterprise AI is bought and governed:

1. **Separation of Model Builder and Model Auditor:** Regulators (EU AI Act, FDA, OCC/Fed SR 11-7 model risk management) and enterprise CISOs increasingly require that the entity certifying an AI system's rubric scores, hallucination rates, and red-team resilience be **independent** of the foundation-model provider and free from hyperscaler ownership conflicts (a fatal blow to Meta-backed Scale AI).
2. **Shift from Static Benchmarks to Trajectory-Level ARE:** Public leaderboards saturate within weeks due to benchmark contamination. Enterprises now require private, domain-specific golden evaluation suites that grade the entire multi-step execution trajectory—including document parsing fidelity (**Beacon PDF-Miner**), SQL query validity (**Interactive BI Agent**), and citation grounding (**Enterprise Intelligence**).
3. **Time-to-Value Compression (2–4 Weeks or Bust):** CFOs now reject 9-month consulting discovery phases and 8-figure upfront platform licenses (Palantir). Winning vendors must prove production accuracy inside the enterprise's existing `GCP`, `AWS`, or `Azure` VPC in under 30 days.
